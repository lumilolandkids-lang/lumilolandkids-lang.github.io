Create a happy 30-second children's colors song called "Red, Yellow, Green!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle mouth sounds (soft "boom-chk" beatbox, tongue clicks), hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no synth, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no fiddle, no electronic beats. Bouncy, warm and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along.
Vocals: ONE sweet little girl singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The color words "red", "yellow" and "green" are sung slowly, clearly and a little longer than the other words, each with one hand clap. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no instrumental intro.
