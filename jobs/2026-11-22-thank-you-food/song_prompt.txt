Create a sweet, happy 30-second voices-only a cappella children's song called "Thank You for My Food!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle tongue clicks, hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no glockenspiel, no celesta, no synth, no bass, no drum kit, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no trumpet, no horns, no brass, no electronic beats. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, warm and cozy, very easy for toddlers to sing along. Friendly and cheerful, never scary.
Vocals: ONE sweet little girl singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The words "corn", "apple", "carrot" and "thank you" are sung very clearly every time. "Pop!" is a playful sung mouth sound. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no intro. After the question "Yummy food! What do we say? Hmm?" there are two full seconds of ONLY soft humming and the daf – no words – so little children can shout the answer.

Lyrics:
[0:00 - 0:03] Hook (amazed gasp, excited): "Wow! Pop! A yellow corn!"
[0:03 - 0:06] Corn (bright, bouncy): "Corn, corn, yellow corn! Crunch, crunch!"
[0:06 - 0:09] Apple (surprised, giggly): "Pop! A red apple! Apple, yum!"
[0:09 - 0:12] Carrot (surprised, giggly): "Pop! An orange carrot! Munch, munch!"
[0:12 - 0:15] Chorus (bouncy, claps): "Corn, apple, carrot! Yummy food for me!"
[0:15 - 0:18] Copy me (warm, gentle): "Hands on heart! Thank you, thank you!"
[0:18 - 0:20] Question (slow and curious, to the listener): "Yummy food! What do we say? Hmm?"
[0:20 - 0:22] Pause (soft humming "hmm-hmm" and daf only, NO words)
[0:22 - 0:25] Answer (happy and proud, claps): "Thank you! Thank you! Yay!"
[0:25 - 0:27] Lesson (slow and clear): "Thank you for my food!"
[0:27 - 0:30] Outro (waving goodbye, gentle ending): "Bye bye, friends!"
