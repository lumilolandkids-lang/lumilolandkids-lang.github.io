Create a gentle, happy 30-second voices-only a cappella children's song called "The Sun Melts the Ice!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle tongue clicks, hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no sleigh bells, no xylophone, no glockenspiel, no celesta, no synth, no bass, no drum kit, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no trumpet, no horns, no brass, no electronic beats. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along. Friendly and cheerful, never scary.
Vocals: ONE sweet little girl singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The words "ice", "cold", "melt" and "sun" are sung clearly every time. "Drip, drip, drip" is sung as light, bouncy, staccato notes. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no intro. After the question "What melts the ice? Hmm?" there are two full seconds of ONLY soft humming and the daf – no words – so little children can shout the answer.

Lyrics:
[0:00 - 0:03] Hook (surprised gasp, a little worried): "Oh no! My duck is in the ice!"
[0:03 - 0:06] Touch (giggly, a little shiver): "Ice is cold! Cold, cold, cold!"
[0:06 - 0:09] Sun (warm and bright, looking up): "Warm sun, shine, shine, shine!"
[0:09 - 0:12] Chorus (bouncy, claps): "Drip, drip, drip! The ice melts!"
[0:12 - 0:15] Copy me (giggly, going lower): "Melt with me! Down, down, down!"
[0:15 - 0:18] Payoff (big happy cheer): "Hooray! My duck is free!"
[0:18 - 0:20] Question (slow and curious, to the listener): "What melts the ice? Hmm?"
[0:20 - 0:22] Pause (soft humming "hmm-hmm" and daf only, NO words)
[0:22 - 0:25] Answer (happy and proud, claps): "The sun! The warm sun! Yay!"
[0:25 - 0:27] Lesson (slow and clear): "Ice melts into water!"
[0:27 - 0:30] Outro (waving goodbye, gentle ending): "Bye bye, friends!"
