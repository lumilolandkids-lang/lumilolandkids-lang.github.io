Create a sweet, happy 30-second voices-only a cappella children's song called "Wash Your Hands!".

ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle tongue clicks, hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, warm and playful, very easy for toddlers to sing along. Friendly and cheerful, never scary.
Vocals: ONE sweet little girl singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The words "soap", "water", "towel", "wash" and "hands" are sung very clearly every time. "Splash, splash" and "pat, pat" are playful sung mouth sounds. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no intro. After the question "Before we eat, what do we do? Hmm?" there are two full seconds of ONLY soft humming and the daf – no words – so little children can shout the answer.
