Create a happy 30-second children's opposites song called "Big and Small!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle mouth sounds (soft "boom-chk" beatbox, tongue clicks), hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no glockenspiel, no synth, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no trumpet, no horns, no brass, no electronic beats. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along. Friendly and cheerful, never scary.
Vocals: ONE sweet little girl singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The word "BIG" is sung with a big, round, wide voice and one strong clap; the word "small" is sung softly and lightly with a tiny finger snap, so the opposite is easy to hear. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no instrumental intro.
