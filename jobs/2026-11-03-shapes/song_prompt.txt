Create a happy 30-second children's shapes song called "Circle, Square, Triangle!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle mouth sounds (soft "boom-chk" beatbox, tongue clicks), hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no synth, no strings, no flute, no electronic beats. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along.
Vocals: ONE sweet little boy singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. Every shape name sung slowly and clearly; one hand clap on each number when he counts the sides. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no instrumental intro.

Lyrics:
[0:00 - 0:03] Hook (surprised and giggly): "Wow! Look! A circle!"
[0:03 - 0:07] Copy-me (slowly, like drawing a circle in the air): "Circle, circle, round and round! Draw it with me, round and round!"
[0:07 - 0:11] Shape 2 (bouncy, a clap on each number): "Square, square! One, two, three, four sides!"
[0:11 - 0:15] Shape 3 (bouncy, a clap on each number): "Triangle, triangle! One, two, three sides!"
[0:15 - 0:18] Review (one clap on each shape): "Circle! Square! Triangle!"

[0:22 - 0:25] Answer (happy and proud, claps): "There it is! The triangle! Yay!"
[0:25 - 0:28] Lesson (gently): "Shapes, shapes, shapes are everywhere!"
[0:28 - 0:30] Outro (waving goodbye, gentle ending): "Bye bye, friends!"
