Create a gentle, happy 30-second voices-only a cappella children's song called "Float or Sink?".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no glockenspiel, no synth, no bass, no drum kit, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no trumpet, no horns, no brass, no electronic beats. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along. Friendly and cheerful, never scary.
Vocals: ONE sweet little boy singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The words "float" and "sink" are sung clearly every time: "float" on a light, rising note; "sink" and "down, down, down" on a gentle falling melody. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no intro. After the question "Float or sink? Hmm?" there are two full seconds of ONLY soft humming and the daf – no words – so little children can shout the answer.

[0:24 - 0:27] Lesson (slow and proud): "Duck and apple float! The rock sinks!"
[0:27 - 0:30] Outro (waving goodbye, gentle ending): "Float and sink! Bye bye, friends!"
