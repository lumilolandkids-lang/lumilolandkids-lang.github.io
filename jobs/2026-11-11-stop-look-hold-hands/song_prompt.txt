Create a happy 30-second children's safety song called "Stop! Look! Hold Hands!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle mouth sounds (soft "boom-chk" beatbox, tongue clicks), hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no synth, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no fiddle, no trumpet, no horns, no brass, no electronic beats. Bouncy, warm and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along. Friendly and reassuring, never scary.
Vocals: ONE sweet little boy singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The three safety words "Stop!", "Look!" and "Hold hands!" are sung slowly, clearly and proudly, each with one hand clap. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no instrumental intro.

[0:06 - 0:09] Step 1 (firm but cute, hands up): "Stop at the edge! Hands up, stop!"

[0:23 - 0:26] Answer (happy and proud, claps): "Stop! Look! Hold hands! Yay!"

[0:28 - 0:30] Outro (waving goodbye, gentle ending): "Bye bye, friends!"
