Create a sweet, happy 30-second voices-only a cappella children's song called "Blue, Orange, Purple!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle tongue clicks, hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no glockenspiel, no celesta, no synth, no bass, no drum kit, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no trumpet, no horns, no brass, no electronic beats. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, warm and playful, very easy for toddlers to sing along. Friendly and cheerful, never scary.
Vocals: ONE sweet little boy singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The color words "blue", "orange" and "purple" are sung very slowly and clearly every time. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no intro. After the question "Which one is purple? Hmm?" there are two full seconds of ONLY soft humming and the daf – no words – so little children can shout the answer.
