Create a happy 30-second children's animal sounds song called "Moo, Baa, Cluck!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle mouth sounds (soft "boom-chk" beatbox, tongue clicks), hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no synth, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no fiddle, no electronic beats. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along.
Vocals: ONE sweet little boy singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. Every animal name and every animal sound sung slowly and clearly, the animal sounds playful and long ("moooo", "baaaa"); one hand clap on each sound in the review line. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no instrumental intro.

Lyrics:
[0:00 - 0:03] Hook (surprised and giggly): "Wow! A big cow! Moo, moo!"
[0:03 - 0:07] Animal 1 + say it with me (slow, round "moo"): "The cow says moo! Say it with me: moo, moo!"
[0:07 - 0:11] Animal 2 (bouncy): "A fluffy sheep! The sheep says baa! Baa, baa!"
[0:11 - 0:15] Animal 3 (bouncy, flapping): "A little hen! Cluck, cluck, cluck! Flap your wings with me!"
[0:15 - 0:18] Review (one clap on each sound): "Moo! Baa! Cluck, cluck!"
[0:18 - 0:20] Question (slow and curious): "Who says moo? Hmm?"
[0:20 - 0:22] Pause (soft humming "hmm-hmm" and daf only, no words – time for kids to answer)
[0:22 - 0:25] Answer (happy and proud, claps): "The cow! Moo, moo! Yay!"
[0:25 - 0:28] Lesson (gently): "Every animal has its own sound!"
[0:28 - 0:30] Outro (waving goodbye, gentle ending): "Bye bye, friends!"
