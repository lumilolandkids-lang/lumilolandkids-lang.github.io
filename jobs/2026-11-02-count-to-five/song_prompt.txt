Create a happy 30-second children's counting song called "Count to Five!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle mouth sounds (soft "boom-chk" beatbox, tongue clicks), hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no synth, no strings, no flute, no electronic beats. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along.
Vocals: a sweet little boy and a sweet little girl singing, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English; the boy sings the hook, the girl answers kindly and asks the question, the counting and the chorus are sung together. Every number sung slowly and clearly, one number per beat with one hand clap on each number. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no instrumental intro.

Lyrics:
[0:00 - 0:04] Hook (boy, surprised): "Oh no! Oh no! My tower fell down!"
[0:04 - 0:08] Verse (girl, kind and cheerful): "Don't be sad! Let's build it again!"
[0:08 - 0:13] Counting (together, slowly, a clap on each number): "One... two... three... four... five!"
[0:13 - 0:16] Question (girl, slow and curious): "How many blocks? Hmm?"
[0:16 - 0:18] Pause (soft humming "hmm-hmm" and daf only, no words – time for kids to answer)
[0:18 - 0:22] Answer (together, happy): "Five! Five blocks! Hooray!"
[0:22 - 0:26] Copy-me (together, a clap on each number): "Clap and count! One, two, three, four, five!"
[0:26 - 0:30] Outro (together, waving goodbye, gentle ending): "Five blocks tall! Bye bye, friends!"
