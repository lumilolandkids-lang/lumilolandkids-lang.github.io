Create a happy 30-second children's safety song called "Hot, Hot! Don't Touch!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle mouth sounds (soft "boom-chk" beatbox, tongue clicks), hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no synth, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no fiddle, no electronic beats. Bouncy, warm and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along.
Vocals: ONE sweet little girl singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The words "hot", "don't touch", "wait" and "blow" are sung slowly and clearly; the blowing sound "whoo" is soft and long like gently blowing on warm milk; one hand clap on each phrase of the chorus line. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no instrumental intro.

Lyrics:
[0:00 - 0:03] Hook (surprised little gasp, then giggly): "Oh! Look, steam! It's hot, hot, hot!"
[0:03 - 0:07] Step 1 + copy me (hands on tummy): "Hot, hot! Don't touch! Hands on your tummy!"
[0:07 - 0:11] Step 2 (sweetly calling Mommy, then patient): "Mommy, is it hot? Wait, wait, wait!"
[0:11 - 0:15] Step 3 + copy me (playful, soft long blowing): "Blow with me! Whoo, whoo, whoo!"
[0:15 - 0:18] Chorus (one clap on each phrase): "Hot, hot! Don't touch! Wait and blow!"
[0:18 - 0:20] Question (slow and curious): "Is it hot? Do we touch? Hmm?"
[0:20 - 0:22] Pause (soft humming "hmm-hmm" and daf only, no words – time for kids to answer)
[0:22 - 0:25] Answer (happy and proud, claps): "No, no! We wait and blow! Yay!"
[0:25 - 0:28] Lesson (gently): "Hot? Don't touch! Ask a grown-up!"
[0:28 - 0:30] Outro (waving goodbye, gentle ending): "Bye bye, friends!"
