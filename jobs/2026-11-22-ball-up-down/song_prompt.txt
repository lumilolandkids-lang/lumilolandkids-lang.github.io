Create a bouncy, happy 30-second voices-only a cappella children's song called "Up and Down, Boing Boing!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle tongue clicks, hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no glockenspiel, no celesta, no synth, no bass, no drum kit, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no trumpet, no horns, no brass, no electronic beats. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along. Friendly and cheerful, never scary.
Vocals: ONE sweet little boy singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The words "up" and "down" are sung very clearly every time: "up" on a rising, high note and "down" on a falling, low note, so the melody itself goes up and down. "Boing" is a playful, bouncy sung sound made with the voice. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no intro. After the question "Is my ball up or down? Hmm?" there are two full seconds of ONLY soft humming and the daf – no words – so little children can shout the answer.

Lyrics:
[0:00 - 0:03] Hook (amazed gasp, excited): "Wow! Boing! My ball goes up!"
[0:03 - 0:06] Up (rising, bright): "Up, up, up! Up so high!"
[0:06 - 0:09] Copy me (cheerful, arms up): "Hands up high! Up with me!"
[0:09 - 0:12] Down (falling, giggly): "Down it comes! Down, down, down!"
[0:12 - 0:15] Copy me (playful, patting): "Hands down low! Pat, pat, pat!"
[0:15 - 0:18] Chorus (bouncy, claps): "Up and down! Up and down! Boing, boing!"
[0:18 - 0:20] Question (slow and curious, to the listener): "Is my ball up or down? Hmm?"
[0:20 - 0:22] Pause (soft humming "hmm-hmm" and daf only, NO words)
[0:22 - 0:25] Answer (happy and proud, claps): "Up! It's up high! Yay!"
[0:25 - 0:27] Lesson (slow and clear): "Up and down! Up and down!"
[0:27 - 0:30] Outro (waving goodbye, gentle ending): "Bye bye, friends!"
