Create a sweet, happy 30-second voices-only a cappella children's song called "Open, Close!".

ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle tongue clicks, hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. Like a gentle nursery rhyme sung at home: warm, joyful and light, about 110 BPM, major key, warm and playful, very easy for toddlers to sing along. Friendly and cheerful, never scary.
Vocals: ONE sweet little boy singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The words "open" and "close" are sung very slowly and clearly, each one on its own beat: "open" bright and rising, "close" soft and falling. Hmm?" there are two full seconds of ONLY soft humming and the daf – no words – so little children can shout the answer; the answer "Closed! Closed! Yay!" comes only at 0:22.
