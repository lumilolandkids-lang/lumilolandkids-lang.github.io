Privacy Policy – Video Autopilot

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Video Autopilot is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube scope needed to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and tags. It does not read, store or share any other Google user data.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored privately and used only to upload the owner's videos. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
