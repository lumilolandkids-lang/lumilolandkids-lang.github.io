The animal sounds are SUNG by the child's voice (no real animal recordings, no trumpet instrument). Bouncy, warm and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along.
Vocals: ONE sweet little girl singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing.
