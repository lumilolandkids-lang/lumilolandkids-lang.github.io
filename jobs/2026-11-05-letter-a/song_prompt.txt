Create a happy 30-second children's alphabet song called "A Is for Apple!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle mouth sounds (soft "boom-chk" beatbox, tongue clicks), hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no synth, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no fiddle, no electronic beats. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along.
Vocals: ONE sweet little boy singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The letter name is sung "A" (ay). The letter SOUND "a, a, a" is the short phonics sound at the start of "apple" and "ant" (like "a" in "cat"), short and bright, never "ay". Sing "apple" and "ant" slowly and clearly, stretching the first sound ("a-a-apple", "a-a-ant"). One hand clap on each word in the review line. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no instrumental intro.

Lyrics:
[0:00 - 0:03] Hook (surprised and giggly): "Wow! Look! A big letter A!"
[0:03 - 0:07] Letter sound + say it with me (short phonics "a"): "A says a, a, a! Say it with me: a, a, a!"
[0:07 - 0:10] Copy-me (drawing the letter in the air, slow and clear): "Draw an A with me: up, down, across!"
[0:10 - 0:13] Word 1 (happy, bouncy): "A is for apple! A-a-apple!"
[0:13 - 0:16] Word 2 (playful, tiny voice): "A is for ant! A-a-ant!"
[0:16 - 0:19] Review (one clap on each word): "A! Apple! Ant! A, a, a!"
[0:19 - 0:21] Question (slow and curious): "What does A say? Hmm?"
[0:21 - 0:23] Pause (soft humming "hmm-hmm" and daf only, no words – time for kids to answer)
[0:23 - 0:26] Answer (happy and proud, claps): "A says a, a, a! Yay!"
[0:26 - 0:28] Lesson (gently): "A is for apple and ant!"
[0:28 - 0:30] Outro (waving goodbye, gentle ending): "Bye bye, friends!"
