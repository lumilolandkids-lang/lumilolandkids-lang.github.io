Create a bouncy, happy 30-second voices-only a cappella children's counting song called "Ten Little Oranges, Count to Ten!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle tongue clicks, hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no glockenspiel, no celesta, no synth, no bass, no drum kit, no strings, no flute, no banjo, no trumpet, no horns, no brass, no electronic beats. Bouncy and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along. Friendly and cheerful, never scary.
Vocals: ONE sweet little boy singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. Every number word from one to ten is sung very clearly and separately, one number per beat, so a toddler can count along; the melody steps UP a little on every number. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no intro. After the question "How many oranges? Hmm?" there are two full seconds of ONLY soft humming and the daf - no words - so little children can shout the answer.

[0:18 - 0:20] Pause (soft humming "hmm-hmm" and daf only, NO words)
[0:20 - 0:23] Answer (proud and happy, with claps): "Ten! Ten little oranges! Yay!"
