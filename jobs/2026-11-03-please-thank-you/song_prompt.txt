Create a happy 30-second children's manners song called "Please and Thank You!".
Style: a cappella children's song. ONLY children's voices singing a simple catchy melody, soft humming and "la la la" harmonies, gentle mouth sounds (soft "boom-chk" beatbox, tongue clicks), hand claps, finger snaps and a hand drum (daf / frame drum) rhythm. NO instruments: no piano, no guitar, no ukulele, no bells, no xylophone, no synth, no strings, no flute, no electronic beats. Bouncy, warm and joyful, about 110 BPM, major key, very easy for toddlers to sing along.
Vocals: ONE sweet little girl singing solo, high-pitched, slowly and clearly in simple English, with soft children's humming and "la la" backing. The words "please" and "thank you" are always sung slowly, sweetly and very clearly. The first line starts right away at 0:00 with no instrumental intro.
